Privacy policy

Updated 14 October 2026. Tempo is the chess learning website at chessplayground.app, maintained by HasanAJ. For privacy questions or requests, contact the maintainer through the project’s issue tracker. Do not post private information in public issues.

Your chess games

Chess positions, move history, saved games and difficulty settings are processed in your browser and saved in your device’s local storage. We do not send your games, moves or positions to Google Analytics. You can remove this saved data through your browser’s site-data settings.

Optional analytics

If you choose Allow, we load Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit Tempo, how they find it, and which pages and lessons they use. Google receives page paths and titles, referring pages, device and browser information, approximate location, and engagement information. We remove query strings and URL fragments from the page and referring URLs supplied to Analytics.

Analytics uses first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_… to distinguish browsers and sessions. These cookies normally expire after two years and may be renewed during visits. Google processes this information on its infrastructure, which may be outside your country. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s privacy policy.

Advertising storage, advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in our tag. We do not send account identifiers or chess content. Event and user-level data is retained under the Analytics property’s retention setting; aggregated reports may be retained longer.

Your choice

Analytics is off until you choose Allow. Decline keeps Google’s analytics script from loading. You can change your choice using Analytics settings in the footer of any chess page. Withdrawing permission stops further analytics collection and removes accessible Analytics cookies on this site. It does not erase data already collected by Google.

We store your choice in local storage under tempo-analytics-consent-v1. If your browser blocks storage, your choice applies to the current visit and you will be asked again after a reload. Analytics permission is optional; all chess features work when you decline.

Hosting and fonts

Cloudflare hosts Tempo and receives technical request information, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the website. Our styles load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the network information needed to serve those fonts. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Google Fonts and privacy.
